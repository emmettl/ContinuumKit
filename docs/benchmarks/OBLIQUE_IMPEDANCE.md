# Damped oblique impedance box modes

This unreleased BenchmarkSupport contract checks coupled x/y propagation against
independent references, without importing an application. It complements the
normal-incidence pulse and full 3D rigid suites. Existing cases, pins and tolerances
are unchanged. These are decaying mixed-axis box modes; isolated oblique pulses,
finite beams and measured room/material validation are separate work.

## Continuum reference and complex reflection

The 0.25×0.125 m rectangle has density 1.25 kg/m³, speed 320 m/s and a 1 Pa
amplitude scale. West, north and south walls are rigid. East has constant real
normalized impedance ξ=Z/(ρc), either 3 or 0.5. The transverse wave number is
ky=π/Ly; the x root belongs to the second longitudinal branch.

Use the real part of the following complex fields, multiplied by exp(s t):

- P=cos(κx) cos(ky y)/N;
- U=κ sin(κx) cos(ky y)/(ρs N);
- V=ky cos(κx) sin(ky y)/(ρs N).

Here s=−i c sqrt(κ²+ky²), Re(s)<0, and N=cosh(Im(κ)Lx) bounds the pressure
amplitude. P=ρcξ U at x=Lx gives s=cξκ tan(κLx). With z=κLx and b=kyLx,
the independently solved boundary determinant is
z tan(z)+i sqrt(z²+b²)/ξ=0. A checked complex Newton root in Float64 must
satisfy the residual and passive branch conditions. The independent
[60-decimal roots](reference/oblique-goldens.json) were generated by
Scripts/generate-oblique-goldens.py using mpmath 1.3.0, independently of Swift and
of either application's time update. That tool is not a Swift/CI dependency.

Decomposing the x cosine into incident/reflected exponentials at the east wall
gives R=exp(−2iκLx)=(ξκ/q−1)/(ξκ/q+1), q=sqrt(κ²+ky²). The two formulations
are checked independently. For real wave numbers κ/q=cos(θ), this reduces to
the usual locally reacting plane-wave expression; see equation (5) in
[Sgard, Atalla and Robin (2024)](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/acoustics/articles/10.3389/facou.2024.1414356/full).
The complex modal extension and determinant above follow directly from the stated
acoustic equations and boundary law; they are our derived reference, not a claim
that this is an isolated real-angle reflection experiment.

The positive case has R≈0.361600−0.017346i; the inverted case has
R≈−0.438143−0.008280i. These differ from the normal-incidence coefficients.
The nominal rigid-limit wave-vector angle is 45 degrees; the dissipative mode has
a complex longitudinal wave number, so it is not assigned a fixed real ray angle.

## Independent work and temporal references

Continuum energy integrates p²/(2ρc²)+ρ(u²+v²)/2 over the complete rectangle.
Closed complex sine/cosine antiderivatives evaluate it independently of a sampled
field sum. Cumulative east loss integrates p_wall²/(ρcξ), including the transverse
cosine measure, with a separate exponential antiderivative. Tests compare both
with independent Simpson integrals, and check E(t)+loss(t)=E(0).

Spatial runs use nx=96/192/384 and ny=nx/2 at a fixed finest-grid dt. Temporal
runs hold 64×32 cells fixed and refine the multidimensional Courant number
c dt sqrt(1/dx²+1/dy²) through nominal 0.7/0.35/0.175. Steps are rounded to eight
capture intervals over one real oscillation period; all native fields are retained
at nine captures. Pressure has integer clocks, velocities have t−dt/2 clocks.

The independent fixed-spatial temporal operator reduces one transverse cosine mode
into state [p, wx, wy], where w=ρc u. With qy=2sin(ky dy/2)/dy:

- p_dot=c(w_left−w_right)/dx−c qy wy;
- wx_dot=c(p_left−p_right)/dx;
- wy_dot=c qy p;
- the east pressure cell additionally loses c p_last/(ξ dx).

Interior coupling is skew and d||state||²/dt=−2c p_last²/(ξ dx). The reference
uses scaled degree-20 matrix-exponential action, independently of leapfrog and the
application's semi-implicit wall update. Each scaled operator has infinity norm
≤1/2; Float64 roundoff dominates its polynomial truncation. Synchronous lattice
energy supplies reference loss, independently of the application's per-step work.
Only energy-subtraction jitter <1e-11 of initial lattice energy is clamped.

Initial physical pressure and both velocities sample the continuum mode at t=0.
The application uses the same Taylor negative-half-step kick in both directions.
The implicit wall's initial half-clock flux is Taylor-expanded from the same
fixed spatial operator; subsequent flux is reconstructed from actual old/new
boundary-cell pressure averages. That flux has no exterior production buffer.
This avoids falsely setting an initially nonzero wall flux to zero.

## Source bindings and gates

RoomCAD's temporary consumers compile the current verbatim CPU update blocks and
exact embedded Metal kernels/Grid. Both run genuine nx×ny×4 extruded domains;
z-mean pressure and native x/y faces are captured, with z variation <1e-4. Normalized
production pressure is converted to physical Pa. The actual passive wall work is
accumulated each step. Application production code and tagged dependencies remain
unchanged. Edgerton has rigid walls only and reports both cases as unsupported,
with reasons and no fields/errors or numerical passes.

Each full-history pressure/ux/uy error and cumulative-loss error must refine in
space at order 0.8–2.3 (first-order boundary plus second-order interior), and in
time at order 1.7–2.3 against the fixed-grid reference. Finest field L2 <2%, maximum
pressure/velocity error <3%, closed-face normal velocity <1e-6, modified leapfrog
work-budget drift <1e-4 and initial continuum energy error <1%. Finest loss error
is <2% spatially and <0.1% temporally, normalized by initial continuum energy.

Spatial reflection is fitted from the complete pressure history after projecting
the transverse cosine. Four independent real least-squares amplitudes multiply
the incident/reflected complex bases. No imposed wall p/u ratio supplies the fit.
The fitted complex reflection must be within 0.02 of the analytic value, and fit
residual <2%. Continuum reflection is a spatial gate; temporal runs judge the
fixed-grid fields and loss without mixing in spatial error.

Negative tests check pressure-correct/reversed transverse velocity, missing wall
work, missing fields/clocks, invalid cases and malformed decoded metric arrays.
A transverse oscillator, energy derivative and composition check the matrix
operator independently. The fetched Git consumer checks the public oblique wall
condition and complex reflection.

JSON retains complete native pressure/velocity arrays, cumulative work, solved
modal parameters, source/device/toolchain metadata, errors and reflection fits.
CSV records all metrics. A separate guard checks the exact case/resolution matrix,
roots against independent goldens, all captures and field shapes, recomputed orders,
finest gates and JSON/CSV equality. Dense raw payloads stay in private Edgerton as
verified lossless gzip. No solver extraction or release is included.
